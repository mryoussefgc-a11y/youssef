What is Grounding?

Reconnect with the Earth — indoors

Grounding (also called earthing) is direct contact with the Earth's natural surface charge — the same feeling you get walking barefoot on grass, sand, or soil.

Modern life keeps us insulated from that connection: rubber soles, raised floors, and high-rise living all cut us off from the ground. EarthRest restores it.

Each sheet weaves conductive threads that connect, through your outlet's grounding port, to the Earth itself — so you stay grounded all night long while you sleep.

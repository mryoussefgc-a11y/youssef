Case Study

IPTV Marketing Strategy

How I built a scalable acquisition system for an IPTV subscription brand from scratch.

+200%
CTR Improvement
3.2x
Final ROAS
−38%
CPA Reduction
The client had a quality product but zero marketing infrastructure. No defined audience, no tested creative angles, and no conversion funnel. They were losing money on untargeted Meta campaigns with a 0.8% CTR and 1.1x ROAS.
I spent 3 days mining Reddit (r/cordcutters, r/IPTV), Facebook groups, Trustpilot, and YouTube comments. I extracted 47 unique customer pain points, objections, and buying triggers. The #1 insight: customers didn't care about price — they cared about reliability and content variety.
Analyzed 60+ ads from the Meta Ads Library. Identified 3 dominant creative angles: price comparison, channel list showcase, and free trial hooks. Found a gap: nobody was addressing the 'cutting the cable' emotional story angle.
Repositioned the offer from 'cheap IPTV' to 'never miss your favorite shows again.' Built a 7-day free trial funnel with a risk-reversal guarantee. Created 3 ad angles: emotional story, logical comparison, and social proof.
Scripted 6 UGC-style video ads and 4 static creatives. Hooks tested: "I canceled Netflix after this...", "Watch 10,000+ channels for less than your morning coffee", and "7 days free — no credit card needed."
After 30 days: CTR improved from 0.8% to 2.4%. CPA dropped 38%. ROAS reached 3.2x. The winning ad was the emotional cable-cutting story. Free trial-to-paid conversion rate: 34%.
